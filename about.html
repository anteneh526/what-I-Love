My chess story

I started playing chess about four years ago in high school. It all began when I watched my friends playing, and I became curious about the game. They taught me the basics, and soon I started playing with them.
I prefer playing chess on a real board rather than online because I enjoy the experience of sitting across from my opponent and thinking through every move.
One of my first experiences was a high school tournament where I played two games and lost both. Although I didn't win, it was a memorable experience that taught me to learn from my mistakes. Since then, chess has become more than just a game to me; it's a way to challenge myself, think creatively, and keep learning.

Why I love chess

  • It teaches patience.
  • Every game is a new puzzle.
  • I learn more from my losses than my wins.
  • It connects players all over the world.
